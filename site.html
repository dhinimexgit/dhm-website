About Us

From Powering Homes to Shaping Industries

Dhinimex Co.,Ltd a company established in 2003, has evolved from a trade-based company focused on electrical appliances and water supply equipment into a highly respected corporation with a diversified portfolio of approximately 18 companies across 5 scientific and technological sectors. Today, the group employs over 500 professionals representing six nationalities, reflecting its dynamic growth and international outlook.

Our Services

Explore what we offer

For more than two decades, Dhinimex has been a trusted leader in delivering reliable expertise in the repair, maintenance, and testing of a wide range of electrical and water supply systems and components. Beyond services and maintenance, Dhinimex has also expanded into utilities, powering electrical and water infrastructure across various provinces in Cambodia.
